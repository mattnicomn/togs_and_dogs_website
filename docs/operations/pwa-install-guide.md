# Installing Tog & Dogs on Your Phone

**Last Updated:** Release 8E

You can add the Tog & Dogs Operations Portal to your phone's home screen so it opens like a regular app — full screen, no browser bars, with its own icon.

---

## 📱 For iPhone (iOS Safari)

1. Open **Safari** and go to `toganddogs.usmissionhero.com`
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an upward arrow) at the bottom of the screen
3. Scroll down in the share menu and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. You'll see the app name "Tog & Dogs" — tap **Add** in the top-right corner
5. The gold paw print icon will appear on your home screen
6. Tap it anytime to open the portal in full-screen mode

> **Note:** You must use Safari for this to work. Chrome on iPhone does not support Add to Home Screen for web apps.

---

## 🤖 For Android (Chrome)

1. Open **Chrome** and go to `toganddogs.usmissionhero.com`
2. You may see an **"Install app"** banner at the bottom of the screen — tap **Install**
3. If no banner appears: tap the **three-dot menu** (⋮) in the top-right corner → tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**
4. Confirm the name "Tog & Dogs" and tap **Install**
5. The gold paw print icon will appear on your home screen and in your app drawer
6. Tap it anytime to open the portal in full-screen mode

---

## ℹ️ Good to Know

- **Internet required:** The app needs a connection to work. It loads your live bookings, client data, and schedule from the server each time you open it.
- **Always up to date:** You don't need to update the app manually. When we make improvements, they appear automatically the next time you open it.
- **Same login:** Use the same email and password you use on the website.
- **Same data:** Everything you see in the app is the same as the website — bookings, clients, pets, calendar, notifications.

---

## 🔄 If Something Looks Wrong

If the app ever shows old content or looks broken after an update:
1. Close the app completely (swipe it away from your recent apps)
2. Reopen it from the home screen
3. If still wrong: delete the icon from your home screen and re-add it using the steps above

---

## 🗑️ Removing the App

- **iPhone:** Long-press the icon → tap "Remove Bookmark" or "Delete Bookmark"
- **Android:** Long-press the icon → drag to "Uninstall" or tap "Remove"

This only removes the shortcut — it does not delete your account or data.
