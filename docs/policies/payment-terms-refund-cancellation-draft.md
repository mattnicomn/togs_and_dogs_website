## Payment Terms

### How Payment Works

When your pet care booking is approved, you may receive a secure payment link via email. Payment is processed through Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor. Tog & Dogs never stores or has access to your full card number.

### Payment Methods

- **Accepted:** Credit card, debit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- **Not accepted:** Cash, checks, or money orders `[DECISION: Should cash/check be listed as alternatives for in-person payment?]`

### When Payment Is Due

Payment is expected `[DECISION: "immediately upon receipt of payment link" / "within 24 hours" / "before your scheduled service date"]`.

If a payment link is not completed within 30 minutes, the link will expire. Contact us and we will send a new payment link at no additional cost.

### Payment Confirmation

After successful payment, you will receive:
- A payment confirmation receipt from Stripe (sent automatically to your email)
- Your booking status will update to "Paid" in your client portal

### Failed Payments

If your payment does not go through:
- Check that your card details are correct and your card has sufficient funds
- Try a different card if available
- Contact us for assistance — we can resend a new payment link
