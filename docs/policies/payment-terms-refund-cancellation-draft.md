## Payment Terms

### How Payment Works

When your pet care booking is approved, you will receive a secure payment link via email. Payment is processed through Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor. Tog & Dogs never stores or has access to your full card number.

### Payment Methods

- **Accepted:** Credit card, debit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) via Stripe Checkout
- **Not accepted:** Cash, checks, or money orders are not accepted during initial rollout `[PENDING MATTHEW: Confirm no cash/check exceptions needed]`

### When Payment Is Due

Payment is due within **24 hours** after the payment link is sent, and **before your scheduled service begins** — whichever comes first.

If a payment link is not completed within 30 minutes, the link will expire automatically. Contact us and we will send a new payment link at no additional cost.

### Payment Confirmation

After successful payment, you will receive:
- A payment confirmation receipt from Stripe (sent automatically to your email)
- Your booking status will update to "Paid" in the admin system

### Failed Payments

If your payment does not go through:
- Check that your card details are correct and your card has sufficient funds
- Try a different card if available
- Contact us for assistance — we can send a new payment link

## Refund Policy

### Eligibility

- **Full refund:** Available if you cancel before your service begins AND outside the late-cancellation window (see Cancellation Policy below)
- **Partial or case-by-case refund:** If service has already started but was not completed satisfactorily
- **No refund:** For fully completed services, unless the issue was caused by Tog & Dogs (provider no-show, safety concern, or service not delivered as agreed)

### How to Request a Refund

Contact us at `[PENDING MATTHEW: billing/support email to be confirmed]` with:
- Your name
- Booking date(s)
- Reason for refund request

### Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within **5–10 business days**, depending on Stripe and your card issuer's processing time. Refunds are returned to the original payment method used.

### Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will generally not be issued for:
- Services that were completed as scheduled and delivered as agreed
- Cancellations made inside the late-cancellation window (see below) — though fees are discretionary during early rollout
- Client no-shows without prior notice

## Cancellation Policy

### Client Cancellations

You may cancel or request changes to a scheduled booking by contacting us.

**Standard visits (single-day, daytime):**
- Cancellations made **24 hours or more** before your scheduled visit: No charge. Full refund if already paid.
- Cancellations made **less than 24 hours** before your scheduled visit: A late-cancellation fee of **up to 50%** of the booking total may apply at the provider's discretion.

**Overnight, multi-day, or holiday bookings:**
- Cancellations made **48 hours or more** before the first scheduled visit: No charge. Full refund if already paid.
- Cancellations made **less than 48 hours** before the first scheduled visit: A late-cancellation fee of **up to 50%** of the booking total may apply at the provider's discretion.

> **Early rollout note:** During initial rollout, late-cancellation fees are discretionary. Tog & Dogs may waive fees on a case-by-case basis as policies are established.

### No-Show / No-Access Policy

If staff arrives for a scheduled visit and cannot access the home or pet due to client-side issues (locked out, gate code changed, pet not present, etc.), a fee may apply equivalent to the visit charge.

### How to Cancel

Contact us as soon as possible:
- Email: `[PENDING MATTHEW: billing/support email]`
- Through the client portal (cancellation request feature)
- Phone/text if provided by your service provider

### Provider Cancellations

If Tog & Dogs must cancel your visit:
- **Weather or safety emergency:** We will reschedule at no cost or provide a full refund, at your choice. Fees are waived for genuine emergencies or unsafe conditions.
- **Staff illness or unavailability:** We will attempt to assign a replacement provider. If no replacement is available, we will reschedule or refund in full.
- **Other provider-initiated cancellation:** Full refund or priority reschedule at client's choice.

### Multi-Day Bookings

For bookings spanning multiple days:
- Cancellation of individual days follows the same policy per visit
- Cancellation of the entire multi-day booking follows the above timelines based on the first scheduled visit date
